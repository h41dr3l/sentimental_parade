# Bigger illustrations

Use the same free model. These are not stickers, so leave out the style line above and paste each prompt as it is.

## Door (intro)

> Straight-on front view of a single cruise ship cabin door, light oak with a round bronze number plaque reading 15311, a purple bunting of pennant flags each with a golden sun, a silver lever handle on the right, hand-painted gouache and watercolour with a fine dark-brown ink outline, soft muted palette, the door fills the frame edge to edge, plain pure white background, no people.

Portrait (3:4 or 2:3), facing squarely forward.

## Sailing ship (bottom of the voyage)

> Side view of a large modern cruise ship sailing to the right, navy blue hull with a thin red stripe, white upper decks with rows of balconies and small round windows, two tall red funnels with black tops, an open-air garden in the middle of the ship with a small pink fairy-tale castle peeking out, a twisting yellow and red water slide on the top deck, hand-painted gouache and watercolour with a fine dark-brown ink outline, soft muted palette of navy, coral red, cream and sea teal, subtle paper grain, the hull is cut off flat and straight at the waterline with no water, no waves and no reflection below it, plain pure white background, no text, no logos.

Wide landscape (16:9). The flat waterline is important: it lets the ship sink into my animated waves and rock on them.

## Ship map (end of the voyage)

> A cutaway side view of the same cruise ship drawn like a dollhouse, the side wall removed to show the inside of every deck: cosy cabins, a grand theatre, a small cinema, restaurants with tables and lights, a kids club, a gym with treadmills facing the sea, an open-air garden in the middle with trees and a pink fairy-tale castle, pools and twisting water slides on the top deck, and a red roller-coaster track at the back of the top deck, two red funnels, hand-painted gouache and watercolour with a fine dark-brown ink outline, warm muted palette, gentle watercolour sea along the bottom, cream paper background, no text, no labels, no letters, no people.

Widest landscape available (21:9, or 16:9). Keep it free of text: I add the numbered pins and place names on top, so they stay readable and clickable.

## Floating lanterns (rise on the last nights and in the balcony view)

> A sprite sheet of 6 floating paper sky lanterns arranged in a neat grid with plenty of space between them, each slightly different: tall and slim, short and round, tilted left, tilted right, one seen a little from below, one smaller and further away, warm glowing amber and golden paper with a soft candle glow inside and subtle paper folds, a gentle warm light on the lower half, hand-painted gouache and watercolour with a fine dark-brown ink outline, no strings, no sky, no background scenery, no outer glow spilling onto the background, plain pure white background, no text.

Square (1:1). Keep the background pure white with no glow around the lanterns. I cut them out and add the glow myself, so it stays soft against the night sky.
